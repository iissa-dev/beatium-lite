Analogue to Digital Conversion (ADC)

L2A Slides 5–6

Physical sound is continuous air pressure variation translated by a microphone into continuous voltage. The ADC converts this analogue wave into discrete numerical samples (Float32Array).

Sampling & Quantization (PCM)

L2A Slides 19–27

Sampling: Signal is measured 48,000 times per second (48 kHz).
Quantization: Continuous amplitudes rounded to discrete 32-bit float levels between -1.0 and +1.0.

Nyquist Rate & Aliasing Prevention

L2A Slides 29–30

Nyquist theorem requires sampling at least double the signal bandwidth (fs ≥ 2B). Undersampling folds frequencies over, creating severe audible distortion (Aliasing).

Zero-Asset Mathematical Audio

L2B Slides 58, 67

Digital audio is purely mathematical numbers. Instead of downloading heavy MP3/WAV files, procedural audio generates waveforms on-the-fly via formulas like sin(2πft) with 0 KB network payload.
