Persistence of Vision

L2B Slide 53

The human retina retains visual stimuli for approximately 1/16 to 1/10 of a second. This physiological delay enables the brain to perceive rapidly sequenced still frames as continuous motion.

Fusion Frequency & Jerkiness

L2A 29 & L2B 54

Fusion Frequency: The threshold (~40+ FPS) where flicker vanishes.
At 5 FPS (200ms per frame), the image fades before the next arrives, causing severe jerkiness. At 60 FPS (16.6ms), seamless smoothness is achieved.

Video Codecs & Lossy Compression

L2B Slide 56

Uncompressed 1080p@60 video exceeds 3 Gbps. Modern codecs (VP8 / H.264) discard temporal and spatial redundancy (I/P-frames and Motion Vectors), compressing the stream by >99.9% down to 1–3 Mbps.

RTP Packets, Jitter Buffer & Decoders

L2B Slide 67

The sender fragments the compressed bitstream into RTP packets over UDP. The receiver buffers them to remove network jitter, and feeds the hardware decoder to reconstruct the pixel matrix in real time.
