Raster Bitmaps (Memory Formula)

L2B Slides 40–46

Bitmaps store a 2D matrix of physical pixels. Uncompressed size is calculated as: Width × Height × Depth (RGBA) = 800 × 500 × 4 = 1,600,000 Bytes (≈1.6 MB)

Vector Graphics (Coordinates & Béziers)

L2B Slides 44, 47–52

Stored as mathematical descriptions (lines, curves, control points) interpreted by the GPU. Beatium streams compact JSON/SVG objects rather than megabyte pixel matrices.

Resolution Independence & Scaling

L2A 29 & L2B 48

Magnifying a raster bitmap introduces jagged edges and pixel distortion (Aliasing). Vector geometry scales infinitely because formulas are recalculated on render without quality loss.

Network Transmission & Compression

L2A Slides 31–35

Streaming a sub-kilobyte JSON payload over WebSocket achieves >99.9% bandwidth reduction compared to repeatedly streaming full raster images across collaborative canvases.
